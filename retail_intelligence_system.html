01 / BUSINESS PROBLEM

What is actually driving retail performance?

A retailer can grow revenue while still destroying value through weak margins, discounting, poor product mix, or underperforming customer and regional segments. The project is framed as an investigation, not a chart collection.

Q01

Where does revenue come from?

Which categories, products, customers and regions contribute most to sales?

Q02

Where is profit being lost?

Which segments create weak or negative profitability relative to revenue?

Q03

What changes over time?

How do monthly and yearly revenue and profit patterns move?

Q04

Which products deserve attention?

Which products combine strong demand with healthy margins?

Q05

Who are the customers?

Which customer segments and buying patterns create the strongest business value?

Q06

What should management investigate?

Translate observed patterns into operational questions and next actions.
